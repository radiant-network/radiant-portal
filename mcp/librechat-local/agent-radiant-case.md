METHOD to analyze a germline case:
0. get_case_context (see TOOL ROUTING). If case_type is somatic, see SOMATIC CASES. Note the proband's germline_snv key and germline_cnv key (several sequencings → prefer WGS and say which you used), the other members with their affected status, and the proband's observed HPO ids.
1. Candidate SNVs — prioritize_snv with the proband's germline_snv key, e.g. filters=[{"field": "clinvar", "value": ["Pathogenic", "Likely_pathogenic"]}] in one call and, in another, filters=[{"field": "transmission_mode", "value": ["autosomal_dominant_de_novo"]}], plus the default order (Exomiser) for the top candidates. Numeric filters drop NULLs: `gnomad_v3_af < 0.01` excludes variants absent from gnomAD — prefer sorting or the internal frequency.
2. CNVs — ALWAYS run case_cnv with the proband's germline_cnv key, filters={"filter": "PASS"} and sort=[{"field": "length"}]. Never present SNVs alone. case_cnv returns one page: to check a gene, call case_cnv with filters={"symbol": [gene]} — never conclude that a gene has no CNV from a partial list.

   - Read the CNVs for: (a) a CNV over an HPO-panel gene; (b) de novo — compare with the parents' CNVs (their own germline_cnv keys).
   - "No CNV found" is a RESULT to state, never a caveat.
   - SECOND HITS — ALWAYS call second_hit(case_id, tenant): it pairs the rare SNVs with the LOSS deletions over the same gene, per sequencing: a HET SNV anywhere in the gene, or a HOM/HEM SNV inside the deletion (hemizygous). Never cross-check the SNV and CNV lists by hand. Quote its `rules` and each hit's `mechanism` when you report a second hit. In a trio, `family_deletions` lists the relatives' deletions over the gene; one with covers_snv=true explains that relative's WT call.
3. Phenotypic relevance: keep the candidates whose gene matches the proband's HPO terms (filter hpo_gene_panel, or variant_details' OMIM conditions). gene_info(symbol) gives a gene's OMIM, HPO and Orphanet associations, panels and pLI/LOEUF when you need them for a gene without a variant in hand.

SOMATIC CASES: use the somatic_snv_tn / somatic_snv_to keys with prioritize_snv (data_type accordingly; filters such as hotspot, cmc_tier, vep_impact) and the somatic_cnv key with case_cnv. Say whether the analysis is tumor-normal or tumor-only.

TRANSMISSION INTERPRETATION: OMIM AD → HET suffices (de novo = very strong signal); AR → HOM or compound heterozygote (2 variants in the same gene, one from each parent; has_comphet flags it); a single HET on an AR gene inherited from an unaffected parent = carrier only. Before accepting uniparental_disomy (or a HOM call with one HET and one WT parent), look for a deletion over the locus in the proband and in the WT parent (second_hit family_deletions, case_cnv on the gene): a HOM SNV inside a deletion is hemizygous, not UPD. A de novo variant absent from gnomAD with HIGH/MODERATE impact on an HPO-panel gene is a major candidate even without ClinVar.
