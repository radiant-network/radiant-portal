# Sign in and sign out

Sign in with the Radiant account your organization gave you.

:::info[Before you begin]
You have the portal's address and an active Radiant account. Otherwise, contact your organization's Radiant administrator.
:::

## Sign in {/* #sign-in */}

1. Open the portal's address in your browser.
2. On the sign-in page, enter your username and password.
3. Confirm.

The **Cases** page opens.

If your session expires while you work, the portal sends you back to the sign-in page. Once you sign in again, you return to the page you were on.

## Sign out {/* #sign-out */}

1. At the top right, click your avatar.
2. Click **Sign out**.

The sign-in page opens.

## The "You don't have access to RADIANT" message {/* #no-access */}

Your account exists, but it is not attached to any network. Contact your organization's Radiant administrator to get access, then click **Back to sign in**.
