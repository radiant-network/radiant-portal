# Change the language and theme

Show the portal in English or French, with a light or dark background.

## Change the language {/* #change-language */}

At the top right, click **FR** to switch to French, or **EN** to switch to English.

The button always shows the other language. The portal remembers your choice in this browser.

:::note
On your first visit, the portal uses your browser's language if it is English or French.
:::

## Change the theme {/* #change-theme */}

At the top right, click the moon icon to switch to the dark theme, or the sun icon to return to the light theme.
